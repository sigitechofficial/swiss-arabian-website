Swiss Arabian storefront — walkthrough

Home page

Overview of the landing page — hero, collections, best sellers, and merch rails. This is where the shopper starts.

  • Navigation — comes from admin, according to the country. The menu updates when the market changes.
  • Products in navigation — best sellers of that collection. Live catalog sort, not static cutouts.
  • Search — predictive search. Empty field shows recent searches and an advertising collection (new launches), not a full catalog dump.
  • Advertising collections — merch rails on home (and later on PDP and cart). Insider is in progress.
  • Country change — products, currency, delivery fee and ETA follow the selected market.

Listing page

Product listing for all products, a collection, or a category.

  • Infinite scroll — more products load as you scroll. No page buttons.
  • Filters — price, concentration, house / collection, notes, and sort (newest, price, bestselling where the API supports it).
  • Tags — live catalog tags on cards (Best Seller, New, and others). Not guessed from the product name.
  • Wishlist — heart on the card, for logged-in customers.

Product detail page

Full product view from the catalog API.

  • Dynamic details — name, price, images, size, and notes / family from product metafields.
  • Shipping promise — delivery days and free-shipping threshold from the market. Not hardcoded copy.
  • Reviews — stars and carousel from that product’s approved reviews. Empty state hides the block — no fake ratings. Write a review after login (verified purchase).
  • You may like — advertising collection on the product page.

Cart

What happens after add to bag.

  • Free shipping — progress bar toward the free-shipping threshold.
  • Layer your scents — advertising collection in the mini bag.
  • View bag — full cart page.
  • Don’t miss add-ons — advertising collection on the cart page.
  • Discount + promo code — applied campaigns and coupon on cart and checkout. Totals come from the server.

Checkout page

Place the order.

  • Address — Google Places autocomplete.
  • Shipping method — live delivery options for the market.
  • Billing information — same as shipping, or a separate billing address.
  • Payment method — Paymob (hosted page) and Stripe (inline card).
  • Order placement — confirmation and tracking after payment.

Account section

Logged-in customer area.

  • Auth — register, login, and forgot / reset password.
  • Order history — past and current orders.
  • Order tracking — status and track-by-order.
  • Addresses — address book with Places autocomplete.
  • Profile — name, email, and security (password / sessions).
